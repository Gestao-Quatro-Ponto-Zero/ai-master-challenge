THE FINDING

A customer may lose one plan-tier relationship and remain active through another. Across 2023-2024, 54 of
62 date-derived plan losses occurred while another plan remained active. Eight episodes reached zero
plan-proxy coverage; all eight later returned within the observed horizon.



    LEADERSHIP DECISION

    Reconcile lifecycle and event linkage before funding a broad retention campaign. Review the 60 accounts
    with reconstructed plan loss, starting with the 8 full-coverage-loss/return cases. Run a controlled test
    after defining eligible customers and a verified 90-day outcome.

WHAT IS SUPPORTED

Usage totals rose slightly while the observed account-month denominator was nearly unchanged; that is descriptive
source volume, not verified adoption by active customers. Ticket satisfaction is very similar across retrospective event
labels, but missing scores and unknown event timing limit the comparison. Reason codes can prioritize customer
interviews; they do not prove root cause.


RECORDED MONEY FIELDS

The subscription file includes `mrr_amount` and `arr_amount` for all 5,000 rows; 778 rows are zero and ARR equals 12 x
MRR on every row. Yet the source does not provide a reconciled account billing ledger or a validated
effective-period/change mapping. Do not add repeated contract-row values and call the sum lost revenue. Revenue
contraction remains not identified.

Q3 / WHAT SHOULD RAVENSTACK DO?



Five moves. One measurement gate.
The investment sequence follows evidence readiness: correct lifecycle definitions, reconcile cases, then test
retention.
          Instrument lifecycle

          lifecycle events.


          Review historical cases

          Review 60 accounts with any plan loss, starting with the 8 full-coverage-loss/return cases. KPI: customer-confirmed outcome and
          reason documented.


          Audit transition flags

06 / METHOD, LIMITS AND LABORATORY



Use the model to ask better
questions.
The paper's measures are deliberately explicit about unit, period and evidence status.

CLASSIC CUSTOMER CHURN

Unique eligible customers lost in the month / unique active customers at month opening x 100. This commercial KPI is NOT IDENTIFIABLE
as verified because supplied statuses, event records and date-derived coverage conflict.

PLAN-COVERAGE PROXY

Unit = account x plan_tier. Monthly gross opening-cohort loss = distinct opening relations losing coverage at least once / opening relations
x 100. Persistent closing loss uses opening relations still inactive at month close. This is not product churn.

TIME AND CENSORING

Daily ledger is the source; executive reporting is monthly. Intervals use [start_date, end_date); null ends remain open to the observed
horizon. End-date commercial meaning and intraday ordering are not independently validated. January 2023 opening base is zero: rate is
UNDEFINED.

CAUSE AND ECONOMICS

No causal root cause or calibrated future risk was established. Refunds are not recurring revenue. Subscription-row MRR/ARR and flags lack
a fully reconciled effective-dated account billing/change ledger. Revenue contraction, GRR, NRR and LTV exposure remain unknown.

DECISION EXPERIMENT

After lifecycle repair, define eligible paid accounts at risk of verified cancellation; randomize at account level to CS outreach or holdout;
measure account coverage at 90 days, plan contraction and support guardrails. Report intention-to-treat difference and confidence
interval; avoid mixing ticket-level records as independent customers.
