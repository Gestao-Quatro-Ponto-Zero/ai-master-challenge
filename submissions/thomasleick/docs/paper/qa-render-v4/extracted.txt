The record is mixed - and actionable
Events and plan losses intensified; complete-account coverage-loss intensity did not. That distinction changes the
decision.


    THE BUSINESS FINDING


    In 2024 the event log and conditional plan-proxy loss rate rose, but the normalized rate of complete
    account coverage-loss episodes fell. Fifty-four of 62 plan losses left another plan active; all eight
    complete-loss episodes later returned. This is not a verified customer-churn trend.




    Q1 / WHY?

    No causal root cause is established. Feature (114/600), support (104/600) and budget (104/600) are the
    leading declared reasons to test. Lifecycle indicators conflict across 312/500 accounts. Confidence: high
    on counts; low on causality.




    Q2 / WHO?

    Enterprise has 5/120 conditional 2024 opening relations lost (4.17%); Basic 2/132 (1.52%); Pro 0/122.
    Eight complete-loss/return histories merit reconciliation, not risk scoring. Confidence: moderate on
    date-reconstructed history; low on future risk.




    Q3 / WHAT NEXT?

    Fix lifecycle/event links; reconcile 60 historical affected accounts; audit tier flags; then randomize eligible
    accounts into outreach and holdout. Scenario net value ranges from -$1,056 to +$16,080 under stated
    assumptions. Confidence: high on arithmetic; low on assumptions.




DECISION

Fund measurement and focused case review now. Do not fund broad discounting on the strength of
event counts alone.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Customer churn rate = unique eligible customers lost in the period / unique active customers at its opening. A commercially
verified customer churn KPI is not identifiable here. The conditional 2024 account x plan-tier opening cohort gives 7/374
(1.87%) losing coverage at any time, 3/374 (0.80%) still uncovered at year-end, and 0/190 opening accounts with complete
coverage loss. Use this only as a PLAN-LEVEL PROXY under date intervals [start_date, end_date). There is no product_id; dates
do not establish effective commercial end; account-only events have no subscription link. Recorded-event intensity is rows per
opening account-month, not a churn rate. Recurring revenue changes, causal drivers and calibrated future risk remain
UNKNOWN.
