# 02e: a second look at the colour and at the chart of the window (reviewer who did not draw them)

You review fourteen screenshots of a web tool. You did not build it and you get no explanation from its author. Do not edit any file, do not run git, do not open any other file on this machine. Time box: 6 minutes.

## Who uses it
A B2B seller, not technical, opens it on Monday morning between two calls and has about ten seconds to know where to start. A sales manager uses the same tool to see the team.

## The screenshots
In `ai-master-challenge/submissions/daniel-resende/process-log/screenshots/`, the files `02-` to `15-`. Light theme: `02` (laptop), `03` (large screen), `04` (phone), `05` (mouse over the second deal), `06` (a panel opened by a button), `07` (a seller who only has a queue), `08` (a Monday of the past), `09` (manager, mouse over a seller), `14` (an early Monday with no history), `15` (the list to clean opened on request). Dark theme: `10` (mouse over an age of the chart), `11`, `12` (manager), `13` (phone, a deal tapped).

## What to judge, in this order
1. Ten seconds: looking only at `02` and at `04`, what would the seller do first? One sentence.
2. The chart under the row of six numbers: what does it say, in your own words, without reading any explanation? Is each column's number readable and does the height match it? Does anything in it suggest that one deal is more likely to close than another?
3. Colour: does the dark theme (`10` to `13`) keep the same meanings and stay readable?
4. The explanations in `05`, `09`, `10` and `13`: are they beside the thing they explain, readable, and do they leave the headline uncovered?
5. Defects you can point at: overlap, cut text, misalignment, a number with no unit, a label that does not say what the thing is, something that looks broken or empty by accident (`07`, `08`, `14`).

## Send back (under 300 words, in English)
The answers to items 1 and 2 in one sentence each; then findings ranked by how much they hurt the seller, each with the screenshot name and the place on the screen. No praise, no summary of what the screens contain. Where you find nothing, say "nothing found".
