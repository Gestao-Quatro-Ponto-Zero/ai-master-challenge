# 02c: a second look at the screens (reviewer who did not draw them)

You review nine screenshots of a web tool. You did not build it and you get no explanation from its author. Do not edit any file, do not run git, do not open any other file on this machine. Time box: 8 minutes.

## Who uses it
A B2B seller, not technical, opens it on Monday morning between two calls and has about ten seconds to know where to start. A sales manager uses the same tool to see the team.

## The screenshots
In `ai-master-challenge/submissions/daniel-resende/process-log/screenshots/`:
`02-vendedor-segunda-1920.png`, `03-vendedor-pagina-inteira-1366.png`, `04-vendedor-celular-412.png`, `05-vendedor-so-com-fila-1366.png`, `06-segunda-do-passado-1366.png`, `07-gestor-1920.png`, `08-tema-escuro-1366.png`, `09-segunda-sem-historico-1366.png`, `10-composicao-empilhada-1366.png`.
(`02` and `10` are the same screen in two compositions: three blocks side by side, or stacked.)

## What to judge, in this order
1. Ten seconds: looking only at the top of `02` and of `04`, what would the seller do first? Say it in one sentence. If you cannot tell, that is the main finding.
2. Hierarchy and reading: what competes with the main message; text that is too small, too grey or too long for a seller; anything that reads like a report instead of a tool.
3. Honesty of the picture: does anything on any screen suggest that one deal is more likely to close than another, or look like a per-deal percentage? Is there a place where the screen looks broken or empty by accident (`05`, `09`)?
4. Defects you can point at: overlap, cut text, misalignment, a label that does not say what the thing is, inconsistent wording between screens, poor contrast in `08`.
5. [item retirado desta cópia]
6. Between `02` and `10`: which composition serves the seller better, and why, in two lines.

## Send back (under 350 words, in English)
The one-sentence answer to item 1; then a list of findings ranked by how much they hurt the seller, each with the screenshot name and where on the screen; then the answer to item 6. No praise, no summary of what the screens contain. If you find nothing in a category, say "nothing found" for it.
