# 02f: the fifteen-year-old test (one fresh reviewer per screenshot, and nothing else)

Each reviewer receives only the text below and the path of ONE screenshot. No product name, no explanation, no other file.

> Open the image at the path below and look at it. Do not open any other file and do not edit anything.
>
> You are fifteen and have never seen this. In two sentences, what does this picture tell you, and what would you do? List every word or mark you do not understand.
>
> Answer in Portuguese, in at most 120 words: the two sentences first, then the list.
>
> `<path of one screenshot>`

Rounds are named in the diary with the screenshot each reviewer saw and what it did not understand.
