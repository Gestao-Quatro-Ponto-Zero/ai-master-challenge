# Findings and authority

Computed A01 metrics are owned by diagnosis.json and diagnosis-segments.csv,
generated by solution/diagnose_support.py. The configuration owns analysis
conventions and hypothetical scenario inputs. The Portuguese report explains
interpretations and recommendations; D02 still owns the metric contract.

All 8,469 original records remain in descriptive analysis. There are 5,700
nonclosed records, including 1,403 labeled Critical; 2,819 Open records lack a
first-response field. These are snapshot/recording facts, not verified waiting
time, unanswered-customer counts or proof that priority handling failed.

Mean satisfaction is 2.991333 among 2,769 rated closed records; 1,102 have scores
1 or 2. Three predeclared one-way comparisons found small between-mean variation
and no convincing marginal association under the documented exploratory
permutation procedure. This is not proof of no causes, no interactions, or
representativeness of unrated/nonclosed records.

All simple, pair and triple partitions are available (150 rows including total).
Do not sum across partition types. Combination extremes are descriptive and
selected from 80 groups; minimum display support is not statistical assurance.

# Implications for S01

Validate status/priority meaning with an operational owner before acting on a live
queue. Instrument ticket events and active effort before SLA or savings claims.
The reference scenario gives 29.75 capacity hours/month; downside gives -26.666667.
All case volumes, eligibility, assistance, minutes, review/rework and valuation
inputs are assumptions, not inferred customer-domain performance or measured
financial ROI. No IT error/coverage rate was used in those scenarios.

The [S01 contract](solution-scope.md) now specifies assisted triage, human
exceptions, high/low anomaly records/filters and success measures. V00 still
precedes model selection and I01. No model or automation was evaluated in A01.
