## Selection Criteria

- All options are approximately 70 characters or fewer.
- Every title includes JourneyGraph and a concrete problem or value.
- None implies a causal result, a completed experiment, or an automated customer decision.
- The recommended title matches the one-pager, README, thumbnail specification, and submission drafts.
