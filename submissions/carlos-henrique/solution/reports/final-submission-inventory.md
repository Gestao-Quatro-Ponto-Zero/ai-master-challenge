## Status Semantics

- `READY`: internally prepared and awaiting optional use.
- `VALIDATED`: an internal evidence gate has passed.
- `PENDING_USER_ACTION`: requires an authorized external action by the owner.
- `BLOCKED`: an internal gate is incomplete and must be corrected before publication readiness.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: the artifact or action does not apply to the current submission.

No URL, deployment, recording, upload, Pull Request, form completion, or publication is inferred from this inventory.
