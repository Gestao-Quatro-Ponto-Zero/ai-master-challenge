# JourneyGraph Video Thumbnail Specification

## Copy

- **Title:** JourneyGraph
- **Subtitle:** Governed Retention Intelligence
- **Optional alternate line:** From SaaS Events to Explainable Customer Journeys

## Canvas and Composition

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Dimension | 1280×720 pixels, 16:9 |
| Suggested screenshot | `screenshots/04-journeygraph.png`, cropped to retain the bounded graph and evidence panel |
| Layout | Text block in the left 42%; graph detail in the right 58% |
| Safe margin | At least 64 px on every edge; keep text within the central 1152×592 area |
| Contrast | Dark navy overlay behind copy; white title; cyan or teal accent only |
| Typography | One bold sans-serif family; title 72–88 px; subtitle 34–42 px |
| Text density | Title plus one subtitle; no metric wall or small footnote |
| Branding | JourneyGraph wordmark only; no third-party or unauthorized logos |

## Visual Direction

Use the approved graph screenshot as evidence, not as decoration. Darken or blur only the screenshot area behind text; do not alter graph labels or add unverified numbers. Keep the bounded graph legible enough to signal sequence structure without inviting detailed reading at thumbnail size.

## Accessibility and QA

- Maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for subtitle text.
- Check at 320×180 preview size.
- Avoid thin fonts, gradients behind copy, and text below 30 px.
- Do not expose internal keys, personal tabs, terminal paths, or browser chrome.
- Verify the final title matches [video-title-options.md](video-title-options.md).
