Where growth came from

Services carried the recovery.

Of the 6.8 points of real growth in FY26, services contributed 4.3 — software, finance and trade. Industry added 1.9 points and agriculture 0.6, both steadier than before the pandemic.

The dip in FY21 was the only contraction in the decade; the rebound the year after was the largest on record.
